How I build

Principles that come from putting AI in front of operators, planners and engineers.

Automate the last mile

Every system ends in a scheduled report, email or chat in front of the person who acts.

Humans decide, AI surfaces

Deviations come with their value and evidence; people own the decision.

Constraints are requirements

On-prem models, proxies and data policies are designed for from day one.

Config over code

Targets, thresholds and routing live where operations can change them.

Document by default

PRD, BRD, SRS, architecture and prompting guides on every project.

Govern before go-live

Risk classification and a release checklist are a gate, not an afterthought.
